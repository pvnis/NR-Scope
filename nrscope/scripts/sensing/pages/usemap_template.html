Where the DM-RS goes (all 23 bursts, PRB-slots)

Used coherently = reference stitched + epoch fill + merged (anchor or edge). Usable incoherently = split by a hole + rejected by gate + not joinable. "On air, UE not placed": PCI DM-RS seen on antenna 0 that has no channel values on both antennas, even with the blind dump.

Band used = used PRB-slots over 273 PRBs times the DL slots of the window. LaSen counts all occupied REs with their own known data; 7.1% is the occupancy their best 5% of time exceeds.

Per burst

LaSen (SenSys '26) and this work

Recording: Sunrise SA n78, PCI 632, 273 PRBs at 30 kHz, TDD DDDSU, 5 November 2026 outdoors, 26 bursts of 2.45 s on 4 antennas, tablet 0x7304 downloading; bursts 6, 17 and 19 skipped (antenna 0 replay incomplete). Antennas 0 and 3 used (antennas 1 and 2 decode only the tablet that day).

Per 150 ms window the reference UE is the one with the most 4-PRB snapshots (the tablet whenever it is there). Reference: epoch templates and joint 4-antenna PRG stitching; groups cut off from the template's main run by a hole have an unknown phase ("split by a hole"); groups missing inside a snapshot but present in its epoch template are filled from other slots ("epoch fill"). Other UEs: self-stitched, then one number from the nearest reference snapshot within 10 ms sharing at least 4 groups; kept if the anchor fit residual is under 30%, else "rejected by gate"; no anchor but touching the reference in the slot: edge matching; otherwise "not joinable". Periodic lanes: TRS (symbols 4 and 8) and CSI-RS on symbol 13 as detected by the survey (score at least 0.6).
